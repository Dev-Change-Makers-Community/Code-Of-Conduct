# 📜 DevChangeMakers Community Code of Conduct

DevChangeMakers is a space to learn, build and grow together. By joining our community, groups or events, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.

**Who it applies to:** everyone, including attendees, speakers, volunteers, organizers, sponsors and partners. It applies at our events and in all our online spaces, including Discord, Facebook groups, WhatsApp groups and event chats.

## 1. 🤝 Be respectful
Treat everyone with kindness and respect, regardless of gender, age, ethnicity, religion, disability, background, university, experience level or opinions. Disagree respectfully, and criticize ideas, **not people**.

## 2. 🚫 No harassment or discrimination
We **do not tolerate** harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats, sexual or offensive content, or unwanted personal messages, whether online or at events.

## 3. 🔒 Respect privacy
Don't share anyone's personal information, phone number, photos or private messages **without their consent**. Don't DM members for promotions, dating or anything unrelated to the community.

## 4. 💬 Keep it relevant
Share content related to tech, learning, events and opportunities. **No** spam, unsolicited promotions, MLM, scams, or political or religious debates.

## 5. ✅ Be honest
Don't plagiarize or misuse others' work, projects or credits. Provide **accurate information** when registering for events.

## 6. 📅 Show up responsibly
If you're accepted to an event, attend or **inform us in advance** if you can't. Seats are limited, and an empty seat is one another student could have used. Repeated no-shows without notice may affect future selections.

## 7. 🎟️ Respect our events and partners
Follow venue rules, instructions from organizers, and our sponsors' and partners' guidelines. Don't misuse credits, goodies or resources meant for participants.

Some events are run with partners who have their own code of conduct, such as the Linux Foundation for AAIF events. At those events, **both codes apply**.

## 8. 🛡️ Protect the community
Don't use DevChangeMakers spaces to promote events, courses or groups that charge students hidden fees, misuse personal data, or mislead people about what they offer. Our events are free, with no catch, and we keep our spaces that way.

## 9. ⚠️ Consequences
Breaking this Code of Conduct may lead to a **warning**, **removal from the group**, or **being barred from future events**, at the organizers' discretion. Serious violations may lead to **immediate removal**.

## 10. 📩 Report concerns
If you face or witness any violation, contact us **privately**:

- **Email:** devchangemakers@gmail.com
- **At an event:** speak to any member of the DevChangeMakers organizing team

All reports are handled confidentially. If your concern involves an organizer, you can email us and ask that it be handled by another team member.

---

Let's keep DevChangeMakers **safe, inclusive and inspiring** for everyone 💜
