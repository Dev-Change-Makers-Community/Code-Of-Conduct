📜 *DevChangeMakers Community Code of Conduct*

DevChangeMakers is a space to learn, build and grow together. By joining our community, groups or events, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.

*1. Be respectful* 🤝
Treat everyone with kindness and respect, regardless of gender, background, religion, university, experience level or opinions. Disagree respectfully, and criticize ideas, not people.

*2. No harassment or discrimination* 🚫
We do not tolerate harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats, sexual or offensive content, or unwanted personal messages, whether online or at events.

*3. Respect privacy* 🔒
Don't share anyone's personal information, phone number, photos or private messages without their consent. Don't DM members for promotions, dating or anything unrelated to the community.

*4. Keep it relevant* 💬
Share content related to tech, learning, events and opportunities. No spam, unsolicited promotions, MLM, scams, or political or religious debates.

*5. Be honest* ✅
Don't plagiarize or misuse others' work, projects or credits. Provide accurate information when registering for events.

*6. Show up responsibly* 📅
If you're accepted to an event, attend or inform us in advance if you can't. Repeated no-shows without notice may affect future selections.

*7. Respect our events and partners* 🎟️
Follow venue rules, instructions from organizers, and our sponsors' and partners' guidelines. Don't misuse credits, goodies or resources meant for participants.

*8. Community loyalty & integrity* 🛡️
We expect members to uphold these same values elsewhere. Members who take part in or promote other community events or groups that don't follow our rules and regulations will not be kept in the DevChangeMakers community.

*9. Consequences* ⚠️
Breaking this Code of Conduct may lead to a warning, removal from the group, or being barred from future events, at the organizers' discretion. Serious violations may lead to immediate removal.

*10. Report concerns* 📩
If you face or witness any violation, please reach out to the organizers privately. All reports will be handled confidentially.

Let's keep DevChangeMakers safe, inclusive and inspiring for everyone 💜
